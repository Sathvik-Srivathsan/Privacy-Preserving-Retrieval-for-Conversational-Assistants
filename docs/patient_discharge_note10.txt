Discharge summary for home. Mr. G. was admitted briefly for observation after a fall at home; no fracture was found and he is mobilising well with his walking stick. He is discharged today with a discharge checklist that includes a follow-up appointment with his family doctor in two weeks, a medication sheet, and contact numbers for the out-of-hours service.
