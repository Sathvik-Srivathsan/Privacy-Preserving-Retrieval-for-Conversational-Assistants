Ms. R. began her third cycle of carboplatin and paclitaxel for stage IIB ovarian carcinoma. Pre-treatment counts were acceptable, with an absolute neutrophil count of 2.1 times ten to the ninth per litre, so treatment proceeded without growth factor support. She tolerated the infusion well apart from mild nausea that responded to ondansetron. We discussed the predictable nadir next week: she should take her temperature daily, avoid crowds, and report any fever above 38 degrees immediately, since neutropenic sepsis would require urgent parenteral antibiotics.

A plan for dose reduction at cycle four has been flagged in case the platelet count falls below 75. Fatigue has been gradually increasing across cycles, and she is using compression stockings for mild ankle oedema. Referral to dietetics was placed to manage the weight loss seen over the last month.
